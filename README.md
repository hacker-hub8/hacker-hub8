## 🔐 About HackerHub8

**HackerHub8 LLP** is a registered cybersecurity technology company headquartered in **Bengaluru, Karnataka, India**, recognized under **Startup India (DPIIT)**, **MSME**, and **K-Tech Startup Karnataka**, and certified under **ISO 27001** and **ISO 9001:2015**.

We operate at the intersection of **cybersecurity services**, **security product engineering**, and **cyber education**, helping organizations of every size defend against evolving digital threats — while training the next generation of ethical hackers and security professionals.

Founded by **Anup Ganiger**, HackerHub8 began as a community-driven cybersecurity awareness initiative and has since grown into a full-fledged security company with an active community of **100K+ members** across social platforms.

> **Our Vision:** To become a leading cybersecurity technology company — strengthening digital security, promoting cybersecurity awareness, and developing indigenous security solutions for organizations across India and global markets.
>
> **Our Mission:** To deliver world-class cybersecurity products and services, build intelligent security technology, and run impactful education programs that empower organizations and individuals to defend against evolving cyber threats.
